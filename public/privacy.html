Privacy Policy for Double Flap

Last updated: 2026-07-15

This privacy policy applies to Double Flap (Google Play package id com.dacquery.doubleflap) and its themed editions, published on Google Play by the developer reachable at deeessseven@gmail.com.

Double Flap is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. We respect your privacy and have designed the game to collect as little data as possible.

Information we collect

None that leaves your device. The game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings, best scores, and any custom images you add locally on your device using your browser/app local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

Children's privacy

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
